About the firm

Legal and consultancy services from Pune.

SES Attorneys, Pune, India, was established to provide legal and consultancy services. We aim to give practical and timely advice, guided by our principles of quality, unity and integrity.

We represent clients before courts, tribunals, and judicial and quasi-judicial authorities in civil, criminal and commercial matters.
